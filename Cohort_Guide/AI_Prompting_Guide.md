# 🤖 AI Prompting Guide for Data Analytics Learning

## 🎯 Purpose

AI is a learning assistant.

It should help you:
- Think deeper
- Understand concepts
- Improve explanations
- Explore analytical reasoning
- Practice problem-solving

AI should NOT replace your thinking.

---

# 🧠 Core AI Learning Philosophy

The workflow should always be:

```text
Think First
→ Attempt
→ Ask Questions
→ Improve Understanding
→ Verify Results
```

NOT:

```text
Copy AI Answers Blindly
```

---

# ✍️ What Makes A Strong Prompt?

A strong prompt:
- provides context
- specifies the goal
- asks for explanation
- requests business meaning
- requests interpretation
- requests step-by-step guidance

---

# 🔁 Weak vs Strong Prompt Examples

## ❌ Weak Prompt

```text
Explain correlation
```

## ✅ Strong Prompt

```text
Explain Pearson correlation like I am a beginner data analyst.
Show:
- formula breakdown
- business applications
- interpretation methods
- Excel implementation
- common analyst mistakes
- insight examples
```

---

# 🧩 Recommended AI Prompt Categories

## 📘 1. Concept Understanding

```text
Explain standard deviation using beginner-friendly business examples.
```

---

## 🧮 2. Formula Interpretation

```text
Explain what each component of the variance formula means and why deviation is squared.
```

---

## 📊 3. Excel Implementation

```text
Show the exact Excel formula and table reference for calculating confidence intervals.
```

---

## 💡 4. Insight Extraction

```text
Help me interpret these results like a business analyst.
```

---

## 🛠️ 5. Error Debugging

```text
This Excel formula returns #VALUE! error. Explain why and correct it.
```

---

## 🧠 6. Deep Thinking Prompts

```text
What assumptions does this statistical method make?
When would this method fail?
What mistakes do analysts commonly make here?
```

# 🧭 Problem-Solving Mindset Framework

Before asking AI:

## 🎯 Step 1 — Understand The Goal

Ask:
- What problem am I solving?
- What does this metric mean?
- Why does this calculation matter?

---

## ✍️ Step 2 — Attempt The Problem

Try:
- manual logic
- formula setup
- interpretation

before requesting help.

---

## ❓ Step 3 — Ask Better Questions

Avoid:

```text
Give me the answer
```

Prefer:

```text
Explain the logic behind this problem and guide me through solving it.
```

---

## ✅ Step 4 — Verify Results

Always ask:
- Does this answer make sense?
- Can I explain this myself?
- What business meaning does this have?

---

## 📌 AI Usage Rules

AI SHOULD be used for:
- explanation
- clarification
- debugging
- insight refinement
- interpretation
- brainstorming

AI SHOULD NOT be used for:
- blind copying
- skipping thinking
- avoiding practice
- replacing manual understanding

# 🧾 AI Prompting Templates For The Cohort

## 📘 Concept Explanation Prompt

```text
Explain [TOPIC] like I am a beginner data analyst.
Break it down using:
- simple definition
- manual formula logic
- Excel implementation
- business use case
- common mistakes
- interpretation guide
```

---

## 📊 Dataset Walkthrough Prompt

```text
I am working on a dataset for [TOPIC].
The columns are: [LIST COLUMNS].

Guide me through:
- what each column means
- what questions I should ask
- which formulas to use
- how to validate my answers
- what insights to extract
```

---

## 🛠️ Formula Debugging Prompt

```text
This is the Excel formula I used:

[PASTE FORMULA]

This is the error/result I got:

[PASTE ERROR OR RESULT]

Explain:
- what is wrong
- why it is wrong
- the corrected formula
- how to avoid this mistake next time
```

---

## 💡 Insight Refinement Prompt

```text
Here are my results:

[PASTE RESULTS]

Help me turn them into clear business insights.
For each insight, include:
- observation
- interpretation
- business meaning
- recommendation
```

---

## 📝 Reflection Prompt

```text
Today I learned [TOPIC].
Help me write a short reflection covering:
- what I learned
- what was difficult
- how I solved it
- what I can improve
- how this topic helps a data analyst
```

---

# 📌 Final Reminder

AI is most useful when you already made an attempt.

Do not ask AI to think for you.

Use AI to:
> strengthen your reasoning, improve your explanation, and validate your understanding.
